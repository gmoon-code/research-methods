# Teacher Pilot Guide v2.0

## Purpose

This release is a frozen classroom-pilot baseline for Research Methods Studio. The pilot should answer practical questions about usability, instructional flow, student reasoning records, teacher workload, failure points, and whether the planned evidence is collectable. It should not be treated as a validation study of the competency scales unless a separately approved validation design is implemented.

## Before the pilot

1. Decide whether the activity is ordinary instruction, research on students, program evaluation, or another institutional category under the rules that govern your school and jurisdiction. Obtain required approvals before collecting research data.
2. Decide whether students will use aliases/project codes or identifiable names.
3. Define where raw research data and downloaded project backups may be stored.
4. Disable the optional AI Coach unless a school-approved secure backend and data-use policy are in place. Never place provider keys in the GitHub Pages code.
5. Define teacher checkpoints. v2.0 recommends M1 Question Ready, M3 Method Approved, M4 Analysis Ready, and M5 Paper Ready for Final Review.
6. Decide whether the public Transfer Lab is classroom practice only or whether transfer assessment is disabled. Formal held-out evaluation items must remain private.
7. Run Pilot & Recovery → Pilot readiness and freeze each pilot project to the v2.0 baseline.
8. Test the site on the actual student devices/browsers.

## Suggested classroom launch

### Session 1

- Explain that the website teaches research decisions and does not replace teacher approval.
- Open Pilot & Recovery → Quick start.
- Have each student create the project and immediately download a full recovery backup to the approved location.
- If independent-learning evidence is part of the pilot, demonstrate Save independent checkpoint before any coaching.

### During the project

- Require regular backups at the locally selected cadence.
- Use My Journey for workflow and checkpoint status.
- Review any Methods Lab teacher-review or do-not-facilitate status before collection.
- Record software problems, deviations, and teacher overrides in the pilot logs.
- Do not change scoring rules, transfer tasks used for evaluation, or competency mappings mid-pilot without documenting a new baseline/version.

### End of pilot

- Export student review packets, learning-evidence reports, and any approved pilot instruments.
- Keep raw student research data separate from usability/process exports unless the research protocol explicitly requires them.
- Review incident/deviation logs before interpreting results.
- Treat local diagnostic scores as heuristic process indicators, not validated outcomes.

## Stop/escalate conditions

Stop or redirect a student procedure when it involves unauthorized manipulation of people, hazardous exposure, medically sensitive self-experimentation, collection of sensitive/identifiable data without the required approval, or a critical Methods Lab issue that has not been resolved.
