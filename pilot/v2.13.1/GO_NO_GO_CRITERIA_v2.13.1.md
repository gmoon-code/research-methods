# Go / No-Go Criteria v2.13.1

## NO-GO if any condition below is true

- a Blocker defect is open
- a Major defect is open
- frozen student-facing hashes do not match
- full backup/restore has not been rehearsed successfully
- teacher packet round trip has not been rehearsed successfully
- required privacy/consent/ethics approval is unresolved
- the observer protocol has not been reviewed
- participant task cards have not been selected
- the teacher does not know the recovery procedure
- the pilot coordinator cannot state which software baseline participants will use

## CONDITIONAL GO

Moderate or Minor issues may remain when all are documented and none is expected to prevent completion, cause data loss, expose private data, alter a research-method decision, alter scaffold logging, prevent teacher feedback, or make the task inaccessible to the intended participant.

Record the rationale.

## GO

The pilot coordinator signs the decision log only after freeze verification, pre-flight checklist, teacher rehearsal, privacy/approval checks, and Blocker/Major review pass.

The GO decision authorizes the planned pilot wave only. It is not a claim that the software is validated or ready for unrestricted deployment.
