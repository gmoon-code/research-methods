## Stopping rules

Stop the wave immediately if a Blocker issue appears involving data loss, privacy exposure, inability to continue through a core task, or a severe methodological defect with no effective warning.

Pause new sessions for a severe Major issue until the pilot coordinator decides whether the wave can continue under the same frozen condition.

## Planned analysis of usability records

Keep completion category, time measures, built-in scaffold use, observer support, event codes, issue severity, teacher burden, and open-ended feedback separate.

For a small formative usability wave, report counts/timings and recurring patterns descriptively. Do not infer population prevalence from a convenience usability sample.
