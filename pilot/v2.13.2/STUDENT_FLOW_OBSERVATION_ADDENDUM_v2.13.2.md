# Student Flow Observation Addendum v2.13.2

v2.13.2 specifically redesigns orientation, progressive disclosure, current-work visibility, and the timing of help.

During first-contact usability sessions, observe these questions without coaching the participant toward a preferred answer.

## Orientation

Can the participant independently identify:

- the current phase
- the current Stage
- what has been completed
- what comes next
- how to see the entire route
- how to see accumulated project work

## Task focus

On each tested Stage:

- does the participant find the first meaningful input without searching
- do they understand the current subsection
- do they use the Stage outline to anticipate later parts
- do they understand the primary next action

## Current-work memory support

Observe whether the participant uses:

- the compact current-project panel
- stage-specific carry-forward context
- My Research Snapshot

Record whether these reduce backward navigation.

## Future-stage understanding

If the participant explores a future Stage:

- do they understand that the Stage is coming later
- does the preview help explain the road ahead
- do they avoid mistaking the preview for work that must be completed now

## Earlier revision and dependency review

When a scenario includes an earlier change:

- does the participant notice Needs review
- do they understand that later work was preserved
- can they identify why review is needed
- can they return to the affected Stage

## Help discovery

Observe which support the student chooses first:

- Example
- Help
- Research Terms
- progressive help
- Ask Research AI

Do not tell the participant which support to choose unless the protocol's observer-help threshold has been reached.

## Visual/cognitive load

Record:

- visible hesitation before the first meaningful action
- repeated scanning of unrelated controls
- unnecessary opening/closing of tools
- loss of place after using a modal/Lab
- confusion caused by route/progress information itself
- whether the current subsection feels too long
- whether hidden detail was needed earlier

## Mobile

On mobile or a narrow window, confirm that the participant can:

- open Route
- open My Research Snapshot
- open Help
- identify the current Stage
- continue without horizontal scrolling
- understand Ask AI when available
