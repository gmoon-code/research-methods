# Classroom Pilot Research-Readiness Notes v2.0

## Recommended first pilot purpose

The first pilot should primarily evaluate feasibility and usability of the instructional workflow. Suitable questions include whether students can navigate the sequence, where they require teacher intervention, whether project backups/review packets work reliably, whether the scaffolds are understandable, and whether the planned evidence streams can be collected without disrupting instruction.

## Outcomes that can be described without overclaiming

- completion and checkpoint patterns
- frequency/type of scaffold use
- frequency of revision cycles
- software incidents and recovery events
- teacher review workload
- student-reported usability/clarity from the pilot feedback form
- availability and completeness of independent checkpoints
- availability of human ratings when collected

## Outcomes that should remain exploratory

- change in provisional 0–3 competency indicators
- relationships between scaffold use and later performance
- transfer-task human ratings

These are not validated measures and should not be framed as established gains in research competence.

## Stronger future study

After usability is stable, freeze a revised baseline, predefine outcomes and analysis, use a private held-out transfer bank where appropriate, independently train human raters, and obtain the required ethics/institutional approvals before collecting data for claims about learning or instrument validity.
