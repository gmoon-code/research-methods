# Reported Sleep and Biology Quiz Scores

> **Exemplar status.** Sleep literature records are real and verified. Student records and statistics are synthetic.

## Research question

Among students in one synthetic biology-class sample, what is the association between reported weekday sleep duration and biology quiz score?

## Introduction and literature basis

This exemplar uses the verified source records stored in `VERIFIED_SOURCE_RECORDS.json`. The literature defines the problem, relevant terminology, and methodological boundaries. It does not supply synthetic participant responses, numerical values, or effect estimates.

## Method

Design: observational / correlational.

The complete 18-stage record documents how units, sampling, evidence structure, analysis, and claim boundaries develop.

## Results or findings

30 synthetic students. Pearson r = 0.75, p = 2.09e-06. Sleep is observed/self-reported, so this does not establish that changing sleep would cause the score difference.

## Discussion

Self-report, one convenience class, synthetic records, uncontrolled confounding, cross-sectional timing.

The instructional purpose is to demonstrate alignment between the research question, evidence structure, analysis, and final claim.
