# Language and Participation in Secondary Science Inquiry

> **Exemplar status.** The source records and cited claims are real. The corpus is intentionally small for methods instruction and must not be presented as an exhaustive systematic review.

## Research question

Across a small verified teaching corpus, what recurring principles describe how language and participation interact in secondary science investigation for multilingual learners?

## Introduction and literature basis

This exemplar uses the verified source records stored in `VERIFIED_SOURCE_RECORDS.json`. The literature defines the problem, relevant terminology, and methodological boundaries. It does not supply synthetic participant responses, numerical values, or effect estimates.

## Method

Design: literature review.

The complete 18-stage record documents how units, sampling, evidence structure, analysis, and claim boundaries develop.

## Results or findings

Four verified records form a deliberately small teaching corpus. The exemplar demonstrates search, screening, study-matrix, synthesis, and coverage boundaries rather than claiming exhaustive field coverage.

## Discussion

Small purposively selected teaching corpus; mixed source types; not exhaustive; no formal risk-of-bias synthesis.

The instructional purpose is to demonstrate alignment between the research question, evidence structure, analysis, and final claim.
