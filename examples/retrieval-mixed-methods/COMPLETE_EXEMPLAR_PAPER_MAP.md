# Retrieval Practice, Score Change, and Student Experience

> **Exemplar status.** Learning-science and mixed-methods sources are real and verified. Scores, interviews, themes, and integrated findings are synthetic.

## Research question

How did biology scores change during a retrieval-practice routine, how did students describe the routine, and how do the quantitative and qualitative findings jointly inform interpretation?

## Introduction and literature basis

This exemplar uses the verified source records stored in `VERIFIED_SOURCE_RECORDS.json`. The literature defines the problem, relevant terminology, and methodological boundaries. It does not supply synthetic participant responses, numerical values, or effect estimates.

## Method

Design: mixed methods.

The complete 18-stage record documents how units, sampling, evidence structure, analysis, and claim boundaries develop.

## Results or findings

24 synthetic paired scores and 6 synthetic interviews. Mean score change = 8.70, 95% CI [6.17, 11.22]. One-group pre/post design does not establish causality.

## Discussion

Synthetic one-group pre/post scores, small interview subset, self-report, no comparison group, integration is interpretive.

The instructional purpose is to demonstrate alignment between the research question, evidence structure, analysis, and final claim.
