# Retrieval Routine in Two Pre-Existing Classes

> **Exemplar status.** Retrieval-practice literature is real and verified. Classroom groups and scores are synthetic.

## Research question

How did pre-to-post biology scores change in a class using a retrieval-practice routine compared with a pre-existing comparison class?

## Introduction and literature basis

This exemplar uses the verified source records stored in `VERIFIED_SOURCE_RECORDS.json`. The literature defines the problem, relevant terminology, and methodological boundaries. It does not supply synthetic participant responses, numerical values, or effect estimates.

## Method

Design: quasi-experimental.

The complete 18-stage record documents how units, sampling, evidence structure, analysis, and claim boundaries develop.

## Results or findings

48 synthetic students in two pre-existing classes. Mean change difference = 6.44 points, 95% CI [3.61, 9.27]. Nonrandom class assignment limits causal interpretation.

## Discussion

Two pre-existing classes, possible teacher/class differences, synthetic data, no random assignment.

The instructional purpose is to demonstrate alignment between the research question, evidence structure, analysis, and final claim.
