# Multilingual Students Explaining Data-Rich Biology Questions

> **Exemplar status.** Background and thematic-analysis sources are real and verified. Participants, excerpts, codes, and themes are synthetic.

## Research question

How do multilingual secondary students describe the challenges and supports they experience when explaining data-rich biology questions in English?

## Introduction and literature basis

This exemplar uses the verified source records stored in `VERIFIED_SOURCE_RECORDS.json`. The literature defines the problem, relevant terminology, and methodological boundaries. It does not supply synthetic participant responses, numerical values, or effect estimates.

## Method

Design: qualitative.

The complete 18-stage record documents how units, sampling, evidence structure, analysis, and claim boundaries develop.

## Results or findings

Eight synthetic interview excerpts organized into three teaching themes. The themes are examples of qualitative reasoning, not empirical claims about real students.

## Discussion

Synthetic excerpts, small purposive teaching sample, interview self-report, researcher interpretation.

The instructional purpose is to demonstrate alignment between the research question, evidence structure, analysis, and final claim.
