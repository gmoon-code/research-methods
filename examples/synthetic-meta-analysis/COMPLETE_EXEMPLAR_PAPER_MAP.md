# Synthetic Meta-Analysis of a Learning Intervention

> **Exemplar status.** Meta-analysis methodology sources are real and verified. All eight study effects are synthetic.

## Research question

Across eight synthetic independent studies, what is the pooled standardized mean difference for a hypothetical learning intervention?

## Introduction and literature basis

This exemplar uses the verified source records stored in `VERIFIED_SOURCE_RECORDS.json`. The literature defines the problem, relevant terminology, and methodological boundaries. It does not supply synthetic participant responses, numerical values, or effect estimates.

## Method

Design: meta-analysis.

The complete 18-stage record documents how units, sampling, evidence structure, analysis, and claim boundaries develop.

## Results or findings

8 synthetic effects. Random-effects pooled g = 0.37, 95% CI [0.26, 0.47], I² = 13.3%.

## Discussion

All primary effects synthetic; only eight effects; simplified model; no real risk-of-bias assessment.

The instructional purpose is to demonstrate alignment between the research question, evidence structure, analysis, and final claim.
