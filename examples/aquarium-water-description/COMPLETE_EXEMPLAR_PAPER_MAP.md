# Aquarium Water-Condition Description

> **Exemplar status.** Background water-quality sources are real and verified. Monitoring values are synthetic teaching data.

## Research question

Across seven school days, what distributions of water temperature and dissolved oxygen are observed in three classroom aquaria?

## Introduction and literature basis

This exemplar uses the verified source records stored in `VERIFIED_SOURCE_RECORDS.json`. The literature defines the problem, relevant terminology, and methodological boundaries. It does not supply synthetic participant responses, numerical values, or effect estimates.

## Method

Design: descriptive quantitative.

The complete 18-stage record documents how units, sampling, evidence structure, analysis, and claim boundaries develop.

## Results or findings

21 synthetic aquarium-day records. Temperature mean 23.93 °C, range 22.5–25.7; dissolved oxygen mean 7.60 mg/L, range 6.85–8.19.

## Discussion

Three aquaria, seven days, synthetic values, repeated records, no randomized intervention.

The instructional purpose is to demonstrate alignment between the research question, evidence structure, analysis, and final claim.
