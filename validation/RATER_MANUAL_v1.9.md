# Human Rater Manual v1.9

## What you are rating

Rate the research reasoning demonstrated in the student's **independent response** to each transfer task.

Do not rate:
- writing polish unless it prevents interpretation
- whether the student used your preferred wording
- supported revision as if it were independent performance
- scientific background knowledge that the scenario did not require

## General 0–3 rubric

0 — Emerging  
The relevant research decision is absent, substantially mismatched, or cannot be justified.

1 — Supported  
Part of the decision is recognized, but important methodological reasoning is missing or incorrect.

2 — Developing independence  
The response is mostly defensible and identifies the central issue, with limited omissions or weak justification.

3 — Independent reasoning  
The response identifies the central methodological issue, proposes or justifies a defensible decision, and recognizes important limits without needing direct support.

## Task-specific criteria

Each transfer task supplies a short criterion for every mapped competency. Use that criterion to decide what evidence counts for this scenario.

## Independent scoring

Score before seeing another rater's ratings or any adjudicated answer.

Use the note field to quote or paraphrase the evidence that determined the rating.

## Disagreement

Do not resolve disagreements during independent scoring. Export both rater packets first. Adjudication happens after agreement analysis.
