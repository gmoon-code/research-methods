Usage & Research Data

Keep platform operations and research participation as separate data domains with separate purposes and rules.

Operational data

Future product-health events must use an explicit allowlist and exclude raw project text, direct identifiers, uploaded datasets, and full project backups.

Collection off

Research data

Future research records require a defined study, approved variable set, study-specific consent, and a random study participant identifier.

Collection off
No silent collection. Public use of Research Methods Studio does not enroll a person in research, and this shell does not enable data collection.
