Student research pathway · v2.0.1 Pilot Dry-Run RC1

From a vague interest to a defensible research paper.

This studio teaches you how researchers make decisions. You will discover a topic, refine a research question, evaluate and synthesize literature, design a method, plan data collection, choose an analysis, interpret results, and write each part of the paper with evidence.

The rule throughout the studio
Your reasoning comes first. The coach gives structure, examples, checks, and increasingly direct help when you need it. It does not invent your data, sources, or conclusions.
A research paper is one connected argument
Interest→Question→Literature→Design→Data→Analysis→Claim

If one link does not match the next, the study becomes difficult to defend. The final audit checks the whole chain.
