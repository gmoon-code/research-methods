## Methodological safeguard

The exemplar explicitly treats Petri dishes as experimental units and seeds within dishes as subsamples. The primary analysis uses n = 18 dishes, not n = 90 seed rows.

## Source integrity

The background bibliography uses verified records for:
- Munns & Tester (2008)
- Munns & Gilliham (2015)
- Toscano et al. (2025)

The package never presents its synthetic data as published or observed evidence.
