## Source-derived design principles retained

- Introduction funnel from broad importance to known evidence, gap/rationale, purpose and research question.
- Literature review uses synthesis, critical comparison, and idea-based organization.
- Results report evidence and remain reproducible/traceable without explanation or recommendations.
- Discussion answers the question, interprets anchor findings, compares literature, examines limitations and alternative explanations, and closes with bounded implications.

## Important limitation

The local writing audit is heuristic. It can detect structural and language-pattern problems, but it cannot independently determine whether every scientific interpretation is substantively correct. The optional secure AI Coach is the future route for deeper source-grounded prose review.
