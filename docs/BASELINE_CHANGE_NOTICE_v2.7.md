# Baseline Change Notice v2.7

v2.7 adds optional Level 4 worked-example support.

Even though the existing research/statistics engines are unchanged, students now have access to a complete end-to-end exemplar. This can influence reasoning, scaffold load, and later checkpoint eligibility.

Use:

`RMS-INSTRUCTIONAL-BASELINE-v2.7`

Do not pool v2.6 and v2.7 scaffold-exposure or independence evidence as though students had the same instructional resources.
