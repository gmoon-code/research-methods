# Novice Usability Audit v2.2

## Audit question

Could a student with no previous research-methods course move through every stage without having to guess what a technical term means, what an option means, or what kind of answer belongs in a field?

## Problems detected in v2.1

1. **47 of 113 notebook fields still used generic fallback help.** They had a help box, but the help did not teach the specific reasoning needed for that field.
2. Several important dropdowns displayed technically correct options without explaining how a novice distinguishes them. This included question family, rationale/gap type, design, hypothesis need, sampling method, assignment structure, ethics questions, and analysis structure.
3. Stage examples usually showed a good product but did not always expose the internal reasoning that gets a novice from the situation to the answer.
4. Statistical controls could still present names such as Pearson, Spearman, Welch ANOVA, Fisher exact, paired binary, and repeated binary before the student saw a direct comparison of those choices.
5. Literature controls such as source type, screening status, and theme stance could be interpreted as clerical labels even though they represent research decisions.

## Changes implemented in v2.2

- Replaced all 47 generic field guides with field-specific explanations, writing instructions, examples, self-questions, and mistakes to avoid.
- All **113/113** curriculum notebook fields now have specific help.
- Added a worked **thinking walkthrough to all 18 stages**. Each walkthrough shows situation → reasoning → possible draft.
- Added structured “How do I choose?” explanations for the main curriculum decisions.
- Added decision explanations for Methods Lab assignment, repeated-unit structure, sampling method, and ethics gates.
- Added decision explanations for Literature Workspace source type, screening status, and theme stance.
- Added decision explanations for Data & Statistics analysis structure, outcome type, correlation coefficient, ANOVA model, and Fisher/chi-square choice.
- Expanded statistical option labels so the first visible wording gives a novice clue about what each option does.
- Added an explicit novice promise stating that students are not expected to know research terminology before starting.

## Remaining boundary

The system can give extensive explanations and examples, but it cannot guarantee that every student will understand every project-specific situation without teacher judgment. Ambiguous ethics, causal inference, clustered designs, advanced qualitative methods, and complex statistical models still require escalation to the teacher or appropriately qualified reviewer.

## Audit result

Specific notebook guidance: **113/113 fields**  
Worked stage reasoning: **18/18 stages**  
Searchable glossary: **90 terms**  
Structured decision guides: **21 decision families**  
Generic field fallback remaining: **0**
