## Important corrections to simplified classroom terminology

- A measured variable in correlational research is not automatically a manipulated IV.
- A control group is not the same as a controlled condition.
- Random sampling and random assignment are different.
- Number of observations is not automatically the number of independent experimental units.
- Technical replicates/subsamples and repeated measures are not automatically independent replication.
- Human-participant access does not imply authority to manipulate or use records.
- Reliability and validity require evidence/plans specific to the measurement interpretation.
