# Baseline Change Notice v2.8

v2.8 materially changes the optional instructional support condition.

Students on descriptive, observational, quasi-experimental, qualitative, literature-review, meta-analytic, and mixed-methods pathways now have complete path-matched worked projects rather than relying on an experimental exemplar.

Use

`RMS-INSTRUCTIONAL-BASELINE-v2.8`

Do not combine v2.7 and v2.8 exemplar-exposure or scaffold-load evidence as though the worked-example condition were identical.
