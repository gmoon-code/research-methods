# Release Audit — Research Methods Studio v1.0

## Major change

The earlier Evidence-to-Action prototype has been repurposed into a full student Research Methods Studio.

## What is retained from the earlier architecture

- student reasoning precedes automated support
- methodological alignment
- safety and ethics emphasis
- data integrity
- claim calibration
- local browser operation
- GitHub Pages compatibility
- local notebook persistence and export

## New curricular scope

The app now scaffolds the complete path from research interest through final paper, including topic selection, literature work, design, variables/controls, hypothesis logic, sampling, measurement, protocol, data collection, statistics, Results, Discussion, conclusion, abstract, citations, and final audit.

## Known limitations

- static rule-based guidance cannot assess arbitrary prose at expert depth
- citation helper does not verify references online
- no literature API or scholarly database integration
- statistical wizard recommends families but does not run full inferential calculations
- ethics guidance is educational and not a substitute for institutional review
- rubric/competency scoring has not yet been validated psychometrically

## Next recommended milestone

Develop a source-grounded AI coach that can:
- critique student research questions and methods
- explain why revisions are needed
- retrieve and verify literature rather than inventing references
- require the student to act on feedback
- preserve revision history and scaffold level
- call a deterministic statistical/methodological engine for high-stakes analytic decisions
