## Important methodological safeguards

- completion does not equal competence
- supported performance does not equal independent performance
- teacher ratings are not silently averaged with heuristic scores
- checkpoint captured after prior support is not counted as independent evidence
- provisional rubric is explicitly labeled unvalidated
- no transfer claim is made
- no psychometric interpretation is made
