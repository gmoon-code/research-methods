# v2.1 Student Guidance & Scaffolding Overhaul

## Why this release exists

A first-time researcher should not need prior research-methods vocabulary to use a website that teaches research methods.

v2.1 adds high-scaffolding guidance while preserving student authorship.

## Every stage now provides

1. plain-language explanation
2. step-by-step reasoning route
3. what a strong response looks like
4. examples that model reasoning and specificity
5. an “if you are stuck” route
6. key terms defined at point of use
7. expandable help for every notebook field

## Coverage

- 18 complete stage guides
- 113 curriculum notebook fields with help
- 90 searchable research terms
- contextual guidance in Literature, Methods, Data/Statistics, and Writing laboratories

Students are explicitly taught terms such as binary, categorical, numerical, ordinal, paired/repeated, experimental unit, predictor/exposure, estimand, confounder, reliability, validity, effect size, confidence interval, and p-value before they must use them.

Examples never auto-fill the student's notebook.
