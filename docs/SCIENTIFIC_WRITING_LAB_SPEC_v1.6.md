# Scientific Writing & Paper Construction Laboratory v1.6

## Purpose

Teach students how to construct a research paper from the evidence trail they already built.

The Writing Lab does not begin from a blank-page prompt. It begins from:
research question → literature matrix → locked method → analysis record → Results evidence → Discussion claim boundaries.

## Eight tabs

1. Introduction
2. Literature Review
3. Method
4. Results
5. Discussion
6. Conclusion / Abstract / Title
7. Citation and source audit
8. Whole-paper consistency audit

## Introduction

The Introduction uses a funnel:
- broad problem / construct definition
- synthesized prior evidence and tensions
- precise gap, replication need, inconsistency, or local rationale
- study purpose, research question, and hypothesis only when appropriate

## Literature Review

The draft is audited against the Literature Workspace. The tool checks for:
- evidence of citations
- citations absent from the source workspace
- possible author-by-author summary organization
- use of a source-based outline

## Method

The factual outline is generated from the latest locked Methods protocol when one exists. This is an evidence outline, not polished prose. Students must not add procedures that were not performed.

## Results

The evidence map is generated from stored Data & Statistics Lab runs. The section audit flags:
- explanation/implication language that belongs in Discussion
- causal wording in nonexperimental designs
- lack of traceable statistical/qualitative evidence
- absence of stored analysis records

## Discussion

Students build anchor-finding records containing:
- result pattern
- exact supporting result/table/analysis ID
- interpretation/reasoning
- alternative explanation/mechanism
- claim boundary
- literature source IDs

The requirements adapt to correlational, experimental, and qualitative designs.

## Conclusion and Abstract

The tool checks title claim level, conclusion overclaiming, citations in abstracts/conclusions, presence of method information in an abstract, and presence of a central result.

## Citation audit

A simple author–year parser matches detectable citations against the Literature Workspace. This is a traceability checker, not a complete APA validator.

## Whole-paper audit

The final audit checks:
- final research question
- design
- locked protocol
- stored analysis
- Results and Discussion presence
- causal claim ceiling
- unmatched source citations
- section-specific boundary problems

The score is a diagnostic alignment score, not a grade or validated scale.
