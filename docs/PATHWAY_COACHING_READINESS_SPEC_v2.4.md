# Pathway-Specific Coaching & Readiness v2.4

## Purpose

v2.3 changed what students see. v2.4 changes what the local coach and readiness system expect.

Stages 9–17 now use a pathway-aware deterministic review layer. Stages 1–8 and the final citation/alignment audit continue to use the inherited general coach where the reasoning is shared.

## Pathway behavior

Qualitative work is reviewed for phenomenon/domains, qualitative units, sampling, credibility, coding/synthesis procedures, traceable findings, and contextual claim boundaries. It is not penalized for missing a null hypothesis, IV/DV, p-value, or standardized effect size.

Literature reviews are reviewed for unit of inclusion, search/screening/extraction, study-matrix structure, synthesis logic, evidence traceability, and coverage limits. Participant recruitment and experimental conditions are not required.

Observational studies are reviewed for measured predictor/exposure, outcome, confounding, sampling/generalization, design-appropriate analysis, magnitude/uncertainty, and non-causal language.

Experimental studies retain manipulated factor, outcome, experimental unit, comparison, replication, design protections, treatment estimand, magnitude/uncertainty, and internal-validity expectations.

Quasi-experimental studies are explicitly reviewed for nonrandom assignment and selection/confounding threats. Statistical adjustment is not treated as equivalent to randomization.

Descriptive studies are not forced to invent hypotheses, IV/DV, control groups, or standardized effects.

Meta-analysis is reviewed for effect definition, independent synthesis unit, extraction/conversion, dependence handling, pooled estimand, uncertainty, heterogeneity/sensitivity, and primary-study limitations.

Mixed methods is reviewed strand by strand and requires a stated integration plan.

## Readiness

Mark stage ready now checks pathway-specific core decisions. Cross-design fields hidden by the selected pathway do not block readiness.

Changing a research path preserves entered data but resets Stages 9–18 to not ready. If a protocol had already been locked, the project requires a new path-compatible protocol review and lock.

## Methods Lab

The Methods audit is wrapped by the pathway coach so literature-review and qualitative work are not blocked by inappropriate independent-unit or experimental-variable assumptions. Meta-analysis receives effect-extraction checks and mixed methods receives strand/integration checks.

## AI context

Optional AI-coach requests now include the confirmed research path and an explicit instruction not to impose cross-design requirements.
